At MAZANYA, we believe that access to nutritious food is a fundamental right. The Reality

Bridging the Hunger & Nutrition Gaps

We exist to bridge the gap between policy and people, between hunger and health through data-driven action in low-income and peri-urban areas.

01

Hidden Hunger

Families cut pulses and vegetables first when money runs out. Calories stay, but vital nutrition vanishes into the shadows, causing micro-nutrient deficiencies.

02

Women Eat Last

In low-income households, mothers skip meals or reduce portions so children can eat. Single mothers carry both the earning and feeding burden alone.

03

Dignity Gap

Sporadic cooked meals don't provide the security or dignity of a stocked kitchen shelf. Communities deserve stable food security and culturally appropriate choices.

04

Evidence Gap

Almost no granular data on Chandigarh's peri-urban nutrition exists. Without localized evidence, social interventions remain generic and inefficient.

Our Focus

Three Pillars of Intervention

Our holistic approach addresses community needs through three core pillars of maternal health, security, and agricultural self-reliance.

Food Security

Providing ration distribution and daily community meal programs to ensure no household goes hungry, focusing on vulnerable slums.

  • Monthly ration kit distribution
  • Community cooked-meal feeds
  • Emergency food assistance
Learn More

Maternal Health

Specialized nutrition support for pregnant women, lactating mothers, and infants under 5, securing growth during the critical first 1,000 days.

  • Prenatal nutrition counseling
  • Supplementary nutrition kits
  • Growth and height monitoring
Learn More

Food Resilience

Empowering peri-urban communities to grow their own fresh food through kitchen gardens and training, reducing reliance on expensive markets.

  • Kitchen garden setup kits
  • Organic farming training
  • Seed, tools, & compost distribution
Learn More

Radical Accountability

Trust is the Foundation of Change

We operate with 100% financial transparency. Every rupee you donate is trackable and optimized to deliver maximum impact on the ground.

85%

Program Expenses

Directly spent on ration distribution, maternal food kits, kitchen garden tools, and medical guidance counseling.

10%

Operations & Audits

Allocated for logistics, volunteer travel expenses, storage, monitoring data analysis, and regulatory field assessments.

5%

Fundraising & Media

Spent on campaign awareness material, online donor gateways, transaction fees, and organizing community pledge events.
