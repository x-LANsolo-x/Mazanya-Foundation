Pillar 01

Food Security Initiative

Access to clean, sufficient, and nutritious food is a fundamental human right. Our Food Security program delivers direct, immediate relief to low-income settlements and peri-urban slums around Chandigarh where hunger is an everyday threat.

Instead of sporadic feeding camps, we focus on establishing family food stability. Our monthly ration kits supply dry staples (pulses, rice, flour, oil) designed to ensure high nutritional content, allowing families to restore their household shelves with dignity.

  • Dry Ration Kits: Provision of healthy grains and oils containing essential vitamins.
  • Emergency Meal Assistance: Swift support for families facing immediate crisis or sickness.
  • CU Collaboration: Working with youth from Chandigarh University to scale distribution networks.
Pillar 02

Mother & Child Nutrition (MAA Care)

The first 1,000 days of a child's life are vital for cognitive and physical development. Micro-nutrient malnutrition during pregnancy often leads to irreversible health challenges.

Our Maternal Health program bridges the gaps by providing prenatal supplementary nutrition kits directly to pregnant and lactating mothers. In addition, we coordinate prenatal nutrition counseling sessions, enabling mothers to understand micro-nutritional deficiencies and food preservation.

  • Supplementary Kits: High-protein, iron, and folic acid supplies for mothers.
  • Counseling Seminars: Teaching families about the maternal feeding burden and overcoming 'women eat last' bias.
  • Growth Trackers: Partnering with local medical workers to record infant height and weight metrics.
Pillar 03

Food Resilience & Kitchen Gardens

Short-term aid is critical during crises, but sustainable development requires local self-reliance. Our Food Resilience initiative trains families to grow organic vegetables in their household backyards or rooftops.

We distribute organic seed sets, hand tools, and natural compost kits. Our field team hosts localized workshops on organic farming and space-saving vertical cultivation, ensuring communities secure fresh greens daily.

  • Seed & Soil Support: Provision of highly resilient seeds (spinach, tomato, okra).
  • Urban Farming Seminars: Practical techniques for vertical farming in high-density urban areas.
  • Nutritional Autonomy: Moving families away from vulnerable market price fluctuations.
