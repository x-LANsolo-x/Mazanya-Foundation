Our Approach

Evidence-BasedSocial Change.

At Mazanya Foundation, we believe that effective social change must be grounded in rigorous research and accurate data. Our Knowledge for Social Change initiative builds an evidence base that informs our programs, policies, and partnerships.

We collaborate with universities, researchers, NGOs, and government bodies to conduct field studies, publish findings, and share knowledge that benefits the entire sector.

Field Research

We conduct primary research in communities to understand real challenges and ground-level realities.

Open Knowledge

Our research findings are published freely to benefit the entire development sector.
