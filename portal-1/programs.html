Seven Programs. One Purpose.

Targeted, community-driven initiatives to create sustainable, measurable change across India.

01
Education

Nanaki Jyot

Lighting Every Girl's Future

Nanaki Jyot focuses on promoting quality education, digital literacy, mentorship, and learning opportunities for girls from all backgrounds. We partner with schools and colleges to bridge the digital divide and ensure every girl has the skills to thrive in the modern world.

Support This Program
02
Leadership

Dhiyaan Di Udaan

Every Daughter Deserves Wings

Dhiyaan Di Udaan is dedicated to nurturing leadership, confidence, creativity, and future readiness among young girls. Through workshops, mentorship camps, and creative programs, we build the next generation of women leaders.

Support This Program
03
Health

Swasth Nari

Healthy Women. Strong Communities.

Swasth Nari promotes awareness regarding women's physical, mental, reproductive, and nutritional health. We organize health camps, awareness drives, and connect women with quality healthcare services in underserved communities.

Support This Program
04
Empowerment

Project Shakti

Empowered Women Empower Nations

Project Shakti supports women through skill development, financial literacy, leadership training, and community engagement initiatives. We equip women with the tools and knowledge to become self-reliant leaders in their communities.

Support This Program
05
Enterprise

Her Enterprise

From Ideas to Independence

Her Enterprise encourages women entrepreneurship through mentorship, business development support, and market access opportunities. We connect aspiring women entrepreneurs with investors, mentors, and resources to launch and scale their ventures.

Support This Program
06
Rights

Nari Samman

Dignity is Every Woman's Right

Nari Samman works toward promoting awareness of women's rights, equality, safety, and social dignity. Through legal literacy camps, awareness drives, and support networks, we empower women to know and claim their rights.

Support This Program
07
Child Welfare

Project Muskaan

Every Child Matters

Project Muskaan focuses on child welfare, education support, nutrition awareness, and holistic child development. We work with families, schools, and local governments to ensure every child grows up healthy, educated, and safe.
