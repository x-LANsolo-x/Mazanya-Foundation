Our Origins

A Motherly Approach to Social Development

Incorporated on August 21, 2025, MAZANYA Foundation is registered as a non-government private organization under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (CIN: U88900HP2025NPL011932). Led by our directors, Ankita Sharma and Shubham Dubey, we operate across the Chandigarh region with registration offices based in Nadaun, Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh.

The word "Mazanya" embodies the nurturing spirit of a mother (Maa). We believe that no child, mother, or family in Chandigarh's peri-urban margins should go to bed hungry. We address immediate nutrition emergencies while establishing root resilience.

Evidence-Based Social Work

Food Security Observatory

Without granular local data, social campaigns remain generic. Our research division drives micro-field studies on food gaps and nutritional deficiency patterns in Chandigarh's peri-urban colonies.

Micro-field Analytics

We perform systematic doorstep audits to catalog food insecurity indicators, tracking food availability and portions across diverse family brackets.

Policy Bridging

Translating academic studies and food security metrics into simple, actionable policies that local government agencies can utilize.

Maternal Audits

Deep studies into intra-household distribution biases, specifically analyzing the "women eat last" trend to create supplementary intervention metrics.

Data Transparency

All micro-data will be aggregated into open-source databases, encouraging external social researchers and college institutions to review field insights.

Field Dashboard Auditing

We are currently auditing our latest winter field metrics to ensure 100% accuracy. Our live interactive Food Security Observatory dashboard will go live shortly.

Financial Integrity

Radical Accountability

We honor every single donation by operating with the highest transparency. We ensure that our overheads are kept to an absolute minimum.

Independently Audited

Our accounts are audited annually by certified independent Chartered Accountants in compliance with local laws. This guarantees that all funding utilization stays 100% compliant with standard NGO governance structures.

Live Dashboards

We update our project delivery dashboard monthly. Every bag of flour distributed, every seedling handed out, and every medical kit delivered is registered for public inspection.

Optimization Focus

By employing student volunteers from Chandigarh University, we drastically minimize campaign overheads, channeling 85% of funds directly to family items.
